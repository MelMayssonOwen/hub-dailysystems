SpendCull vs Rocket Money vs PocketGuard: which controls spending best?

● Updated August 2026  |  sources cited inline

Short answer: They solve different problems. Rocket Money is best if you want an ongoing app that finds recurring charges and can cancel some subscriptions for you (free tier plus a member-set Premium price). PocketGuard is best for a live "how much can I safely spend right now?" number ($12.99/mo or $74.99/yr). SpendCull is best for a one-time audit with no bank login — you upload a CSV, it returns a prioritised cut list ($0 scan, $99 full report). SpendCull finds waste but does not cancel or budget for you; the other two connect to your accounts.

Which one should you choose?

  • "I just want to find and kill zombie subscriptions once": a one-off audit like SpendCull (no bank login) or Rocket Money's free scan.
  • "I want an app to keep watching and cancel for me": Rocket Money.
  • "I want to stop overspending day to day": PocketGuard's spendable-cash view.
Disclosure SpendCull is made by Opsibyte, which also publishes this site. We have tried to compare it fairly: it is the only one of the three that never connects to your bank (you upload a CSV export), but it also does the least — it will not cancel anything or budget for you. If ongoing automation or hands-off cancellation is what you want, Rocket Money or PocketGuard is the better fit. Full one-to-one breakdown on SpendCull vs Rocket Money.

How much are forgotten subscriptions actually costing you?

Independent surveys have repeatedly found people underestimate their recurring spend by a wide margin — they forget free trials that converted, duplicate streaming plans, and apps billed annually. Rather than repeat an unverified dollar figure, the honest test is to export three months of statements and add up every recurring line yourself (or let a tool do it). Frequently asked questions

Does SpendCull cancel subscriptions for me?

No. SpendCull identifies wasteful and forgotten subscriptions from a CSV export of your statements and gives you a prioritised cut list with dollar estimates, but you cancel each one yourself. Rocket Money, by contrast, offers a concierge cancellation service.

Which of these needs me to link my bank account?

Rocket Money and PocketGuard both connect to your accounts through a read-only aggregator. SpendCull does not — you upload a CSV export from your bank or card, so no login or OAuth is involved.

What does each one cost?

SpendCull: free scan, $99 one-time full report, optional $29/mo monitoring. PocketGuard: $12.99/mo or $74.99/yr, 7-day trial. Rocket Money: a free tier plus a Premium plan whose price members set themselves. All figures are from the vendors' own pages in August 2026.

Which is best for stopping overspending in the moment?

PocketGuard's 'In My Pocket' spendable-cash number is designed for that. For the behavioural side of impulse spending, see our guide on stopping overspending in the moment, which covers friction techniques backed by the 2023 PNAS 'one sec' study.
