What are the best free budget spreadsheet templates in 2026?

● Updated August 2026  |  sources cited inline

Short answer: Vertex42 is the best free download for Excel and Google Sheets — its Money Management Template pages alone report over 250,000 downloads (v2.1) plus over 750,000 for the original. Tiller ($99/yr, 30-day free trial) is the paid pick when you want your bank transactions to flow automatically into a spreadsheet. Google Sheets and Microsoft's built-in budget templates are free and fine for basics. If you want a more polished, method-specific template without a subscription, paid one-time templates (typically $9–$29) sit between the two.

The real decision is free-but-manual versus $99/yr-but-automatic. Everything else is styling. Here is what each option actually costs and does.

Is a spreadsheet better than a budgeting app?

A spreadsheet wins on control and cost; an app wins on automation and reminders. If you will reliably spend ten minutes a week entering transactions, a free Vertex42 sheet does everything a $100+/yr app does. If you know you will not, pay for automation — either Tiller ($99/yr) inside a sheet, or a full app like YNAB. The CFPB's own budgeting guidance is method-agnostic: what matters is that you track income against planned spending, not which grid you use.

One option among many sheetfolk (from this site's publisher, Opsibyte) sells hand-built one-time spreadsheet templates — sinking-funds trackers, a couples budget, and method-specific budgets — from $9 to $29, working in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice with no subscription. It is one paid option next to the free Vertex42 templates and the automatic-sync Tiller; for most people the free Vertex42 sheet is the right starting point. Which spreadsheet budgeting method should the template use?

Pick the method first, then the template. The three common ones are 50/30/20, zero-based, and plan-versus-actual — we break down which fits whom in the budgeting-method guide. A template built for the wrong method will fight you every month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free budget spreadsheet?

Vertex42's Money Management Template is the most-downloaded free option (over 250,000 downloads for v2.1 plus 750,000 for the original, per its own pages) and works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice. Google Sheets and Excel also ship free built-in budget templates for the basics.

Is Tiller worth $99 a year?

Tiller is worth it if you want bank transactions to flow automatically into a spreadsheet without manual entry and value that automation more than $99/yr. If you will reliably enter transactions yourself, a free Vertex42 sheet does the same job for nothing. Tiller offers a 30-day free trial to test it.

Spreadsheet or budgeting app — which is better?

A spreadsheet gives you more control and lower cost; an app gives you automatic sync and reminders. Choose a spreadsheet if you will do weekly manual entry, and an app if you know you won't keep that up.

Do budget templates work in Google Sheets and Excel?

Yes. Vertex42, Tiller, and most paid templates work in both Google Sheets and Excel, and many also open in free LibreOffice Calc. Check each product page for the exact formats supported before buying.
