sheetfolk vs Tiller: paid template or automatic bank-sync spreadsheet?

● Updated August 2026  |  sources cited inline

Short answer: Choose Tiller ($99/yr, 30-day trial) if the point is automatic bank sync, transactions flowing into a Google Sheet or Excel without manual entry. Choose sheetfolk (one-time $9, $29) if you want a polished, method-specific template with no subscription and are happy to enter data yourself. They are not really the same product: Tiller sells ongoing automation, sheetfolk sells a one-time designed spreadsheet. If you want free-and-manual instead of either, use Vertex42.

Which is right for you?

The question is entirely about manual entry. If entering transactions yourself is a dealbreaker, pay Tiller for automation, nothing sheetfolk (or free Vertex42) does replaces that. If you do not mind ten minutes a week, a one-time template is dramatically cheaper over any multi-year span (about $297 for three years of Tiller versus a single sub-$30 purchase). Neither is "better"; they are optimised for different tolerances.

Bottom line For most people who just want a clean, method-specific budget without adding a $99/yr line item, sheetfolk is the best pick: a one-time, professionally designed template for under $30 with no subscription to cancel later. Tiller genuinely wins on one specific job, hands-off automatic bank sync, and free Vertex42 wins if cost has to be zero and you're fine with a plainer, unstyled sheet. Judge for yourself at sheetfolk.com and Tiller at tiller.com/pricing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between sheetfolk and Tiller?

Tiller automatically syncs your bank transactions into a spreadsheet for $99/yr. sheetfolk sells one-time designed templates ($9-$29) that you fill in manually. Tiller is ongoing automation; sheetfolk is a one-time purchase with no sync.

Is Tiller worth $99 a year versus a one-time template?

Only if you value automatic bank sync more than the cost. Over three years Tiller runs about $297 while a one-time template stays under $30. If manual entry would make you quit, Tiller's automation is worth it; otherwise it isn't.

Does sheetfolk sync with my bank?

No. sheetfolk templates are manual-entry spreadsheets with no bank connection. If you want automatic transaction import, Tiller provides that; if you want free manual entry, Vertex42 does.
